Inter-Bold.ttf
                    Chu giao dien. Ba do dam CAT RA tu Inter.ttf (font
                    variable, truc wght 100..900) bang fontTools:
                        instantiateVariableFont(f, {"wght": W, "opsz": 14})
                    W = 400 / 600 / 700.

                    opsz 14 la kieu chu danh cho co NHO. Instance opsz cao
                    dung cho tieu de lon: net thanh hon, khoang cach chat
                    hon — sai o co 10-12px thi chu dinh vao nhau.
